## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| App (renderer) -> Tauri IPC -> local printer hardware | The printer's failure/success signal crosses this boundary; a compromised or malfunctioning printer driver could theoretically stall or flood this channel |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-13-01 | Denial of Service | `printReceipt` retry loop | low | accept | Bounded to exactly 3 attempts with a fixed 700ms delay (~1.4s worst-case added latency); the retry loop is already fire-and-forget and non-blocking of the sale itself (RCP-02, pre-existing), so there is no user-facing DoS surface — a stuck/slow printer can add at most ~1.4s to a background task the cashier is not waiting on. |
| T-13-02 | Repudiation | Stable-id toast sequence | low | accept | The toast id is derived from `data.receiptNumber` (already-generated, non-secret), so a rapid double-print of two different sales cannot collide toast ids and mask one sale's failure behind another's success message. |
</threat_model>
