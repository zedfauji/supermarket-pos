Purpose: Catch a schema/RLS/RPC-signature dead end after one commit, not after the UI is already built on top of it.
Output: `purchase_orders`/`purchase_order_items` tables + RLS, `receive_shipment(p_staff_id, p_supplier_id, p_items, p_po_id)`, the Edge Function + client contract wired for `poId`, two green integration tests, regenerated `supabase.types.ts`.
</objective>
