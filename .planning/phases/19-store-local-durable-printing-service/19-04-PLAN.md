<objective>
Harden every existing print call site so the broker-submission `Result` is always explicitly handled —
closing the one real silent-discard violation this phase's research found (`ReceiptPreview.tsx`'s
`void printReceipt(...).finally(...)`) and unifying the other four callers (already-correct today) onto
one shared error-copy-key selection helper instead of four slightly different ad-hoc patterns.

Purpose: D-11 makes this a hard requirement, enforced by automated tests, not a best-effort convention.
Output: A shared `printJobErrorCopyKey()` export in `pos-printer.ts`; `ReceiptPreview.tsx` shows a toast
on failure for the first time; `PaymentForm.tsx`/`CajaDashboard.tsx`/`ReprintButton.tsx` switch from raw
`result.error.message` toasts to the three locked UI-SPEC copy keys.
</objective>
