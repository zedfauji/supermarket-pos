<objective>
Build the Turnover/Sell-through report (INVR-04): a query hook combining
`useProductSalesReport` (units sold, reused verbatim from `entities/tab`) with a two-point average
of `computeInventoryValueAsOf` (from 14-01, at `dateRange.from` and `dateRange.to`), producing a
per-product turnover ratio. This is the last of the four report sub-sections and proves D-04's
"build the reconstruction once, reuse for Valuation AND Turnover" requirement.

Purpose: give the owner a sell-through signal (fast- vs. slow-moving stock) without building a
second inventory-valuation code path.
Output: `useTurnoverReport` hook, `TurnoverSection` widget, CSV export.
</objective>
