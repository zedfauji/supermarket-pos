payment method, the sale finalizes through the existing `process_direct_sale_atomic` RPC chain

RPC integration test — the next plan's confirm/dispute UI has something real to reconcile against.

processor, a "Bank Transfer" method option in `PaymentForm.tsx`, and a green Playwright E2E proof.
