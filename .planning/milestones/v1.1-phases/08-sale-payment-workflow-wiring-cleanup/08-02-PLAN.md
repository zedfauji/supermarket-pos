Implement SALE-06 test-first: `process-refund`'s two `const db = supabase as any` casts (with stale "not yet in supabase.types.ts" comments) are removed, and the `p_items` jsonb RPC payload — which today has zero client-side shape or business-rule checking — gets Zod validation via a new `ProcessRefundInputSchema`.

Output: A working, tested `ProcessRefundInputSchema`; `useProcessRefund.ts`'s `mutationFn` fails fast on malformed input without ever calling `supabase.rpc`; both `as any` casts and their stale comments are gone.
</objective>
