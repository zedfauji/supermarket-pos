<objective>
Implement the SALE-05 sweep confirmed in-scope by D-10's "featOrders/wPanels" boundary: fix the raw-Postgres-error-leak pattern in every `featOrders`-namespaced mutation hook other than `process-refund` (which is fixed separately in 08-04, sequenced after 08-02 due to a shared-file conflict on `useProcessRefund.ts`). RESEARCH.md's project-wide grep found this exact `toast.error(result.error.message)` pattern in `remove-tab-item`, `reopen-tab`, `edit-paid-tab`, and `register-caja-entry` — this plan confirmed via `useTranslation()` namespace grep that all four are genuinely `featOrders` (not `featMgmt`/`wAdmin`, which D-10 explicitly excludes: `physical-count`, `EmailReceiptsSettingsTab`, `force-pin-change`, `clock-out-staff`, `manage-categories`, `edit-staff-role`, `RBACDashboard/PermissionMatrix`, `manage-products/CatalogProductsTab` are out of scope and left untouched by this plan, per D-10's stated boundary).

Purpose: A cashier/manager removing an item, reopening a ticket, editing a paid ticket, or registering a caja entry should never see a raw `relation "..." does not exist` / constraint-name-shaped Postgres string in a toast — only refund's leak (fixed in 08-02/08-04) was named explicitly in discussion, but the same bug pattern exists in every other featOrders mutation hook with an unmapped-error fallback.
Output: 4 mutation hooks (`useRemoveTabItem`, `useReopenTab`, `useEditPaidTab`, `useMutationCreateCajaEntry`) each translate their unmapped-error fallback; 4 unit tests (1 extended, 2 new files, 1 extended) prove it.
</objective>
